## 🤝 Meeting Notes

### Weekly Progress

**Key Discussion Points:**

* **Robot Arm Control:** Ogan continued simulation progress, though limited documentation for MuJoCo and ROS has presented challenges. Potential shifts to C++ may be required.
* **Mechanism Optimization:** Deniz further refined the dovetail and pin locking mechanism, nearly finalizing the concept. Buğra proposed an alternative pin system designed for better charging connectivity.
